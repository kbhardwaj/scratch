## Summary

30 claims checked: **27 CONFIRMED, 1 UNCLEAR, 1 WRONG, 1 MISATTRIBUTED.**

- WRONG (#25): the Gee, Jones & Burke 2017 Facebook study is cited across five places (tensions t09, m9 idea 1, c148, d8, m9 hinge 3) as finding "most jobs via strong ties"; it found the opposite in aggregate (most jobs via weak ties, because they are numerous) and only that a single strong tie is more valuable per tie. Fix the wording in all five places; the design lesson is unaffected.
- MISATTRIBUTED (#22): Binmore & Shaked 2010 is a critique of inequity-aversion modelling, not of strong reciprocity as an evolved trait; cite Guala 2012 alone or Binmore 2006 (PPE) instead, in d3, c164 and c166.
- Minor precision notes (no verdict change): Nagel 1995 first-round mean is 36.73 ("about 36-37" rather than "35-36"); Onnela 2007 is one operator's mobile network, not a "national" graph.

Method note: verification relied on search-result snippets and abstracts, not full texts; "CONFIRMED" means the numbers, authors, year and direction in the course match those reported in the abstract or in multiple independent secondary summaries.
